## Important scope distinction
The Unit-02 PPT contains slides 54–61 on network types, network-connecting devices, and internetwork routing (including intradomain/interdomain routing and BGP). The official 24CS510 syllabus explicitly lists only Tunneling and Packet Fragmentation under Unit 2 Internetworking. These extra PPT slides are preserved in a separate page marked supplementary rather than represented as explicit SEE syllabus topics.
